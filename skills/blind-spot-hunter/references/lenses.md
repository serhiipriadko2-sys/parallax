# Blind-spot lenses

- **Actor:** Who is affected but absent from the model?
- **Authority:** Which identity actually grants permission, and can the requester forge it?
- **Data:** Which trust boundary, retention rule, or exfiltration path is missing?
- **Time:** Which source, approval, credential, or postcondition can expire or drift?
- **Failure:** What happens on timeout, partial success, retry, replay, rollback failure, or dependency outage?
- **Scale:** Which safe single-run behavior becomes unsafe under concurrency, automation, or broad audience access?
